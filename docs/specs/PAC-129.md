## 1. Problem

A team owner in Paceday uses the team for three things: finding a time that suits everyone, seeing the team's availability, and protecting no-meeting hours. Today the only way to put someone on a team is to send an invitation that the person must accept while signed in to a Paceday account holding that exact address, confirmed (`handlers_teams.go:316-324`). Until they accept, they are not on the team. They are not in Find a time, the owner cannot see them in the member list, and they do not count anywhere.

That is the wrong shape for most teams. The people an owner coordinates with are usually colleagues who will never sign up, or have not yet. The owner already sees their busy times through their own calendar (Google or Outlook free/busy within an organisation). The owner wants to add them by email and be done. Asking a colleague to create an account so their manager can find a meeting slot costs the owner the feature, and it is also the wrong thing to ask of the colleague.

It also costs trust where the product already misleads: the invite dialog promises a Pending entry that never appears (SF-051), Find a time claims "N of N free" for every slot (SF-054), and a colleague whose calendar the owner cannot read is shown as free all day (SF-032).

The owner's words (2026-11-18): adding someone to a team should not require them to accept. The owner should be able to add them without inviting them to use Paceday. Inviting them stays a separate, explicit action.

## 2. Current behaviour

### 2.1 Storage

- **Teams** (`013_teams.up.sql:1-7`): `created_by` cascades on user deletion (`:5`). Deleting the creator's account deletes the team.
- **Members** (`:9-15`): `user_id UUID NOT NULL`. A member is always an account. The role is `owner` or `member` (`:12`). The key is `(team_id, user_id)` (`:14`).
- **Invites** (`:17-26`): `invitee_email` is free text, with no uniqueness on `(team_id, invitee_email)`. Accepted, declined and expired rows are kept for ever. Nothing deletes them except team deletion (cascade, `:19`) and the manager-domain removal (pending rows only, `storage/manager.go:233`).
- **No-meeting zones** (`:28-36`) belong to the team, not to a member.
- **The latest migration is `040`.** `033` does not exist (gap in `backend/storage/migrations/`).
- **Who can be an owner.** `AddTeamMember` is called with `"owner"` only when the team is created (`handlers_teams.go:61`), and acceptance always adds `"member"` (`:325`). No operation promotes anyone. In practice a team has exactly one owner, its creator. The PAC-25 guard still handles two owners (`storage/teams.go:139-187`).

### 2.2 Team operations (`backend/api/handlers_teams.go`, routes `routes.go:256-273`)

- **Invite** (`:182-235`):
  - owner only (`:190`, via `requireOwner` `:601-607`, which answers 403 on any storage error: SF-074);
  - normalises the address with `normalizeManagerEmail` (`:201`; lowercased and trimmed, rejected unless `net/mail` parses it back identically, `handlers_manager.go:756-766`);
  - inserts a new invite row with a 7-day expiry **every time** (`storage/teams.go:234-243`): SF-012/API-090;
  - sends the email in a tracked background unit (`:226-230`). It is skipped silently when `SMTP_HOST` or `SMTP_FROM` is unset (`:652-654`, SF-066);
  - answers 201 with the invite **including its token** (`:232-234`);
  - takes no display name. The web collects one and drops it (§2.6).
- **Preview and accept** (`:237-334`): authenticated.
  - Expiry is decided in the read (`storage/teams.go:245-256`, PAC-122 SF-002).
  - Accept requires the session account's address to equal the invitee address and to be held domain-confirmed (`:316-324`, PAC-71 granting site). Otherwise it answers 403 "this invite is for a different email address".
  - It then runs two separate guarded writes: add the membership (`:325`), then mark the invite accepted (`:329`). If the second fails, the person is a member and the invite stays pending.
- **Get team** (`:87-116`): any member. `members, _ :=` (`:113`) swallows a list error into `[]` (SF-039). Members are listed by joining accounts (`storage/teams.go:209-230`). Name and email come from the account. Contract `TeamMember` requires `userId` (`contracts/openapi/paths/teams.yaml:1607-1627`).
- **Remove** (`:336-368`): by **user id** in the path. Owner removes anyone; anyone removes themselves; never the last owner (PAC-25, `storage/teams.go:145-187`, team row locked `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` at `:156`). Removing a non-member answers 204. Pending invites for the address are **not** touched.
- **Availability** (`:510-555`): any member. Delegates to the engine (§2.4).
- **Analytics** (`:557-589`): any member. Delegates to the engine (§2.4). The contract says so: "no consent check and no FreeBusy fallback" (`teams.yaml:1027-1035`).
- **List teams** (`storage/teams.go:65-85`): only teams where the account has a membership row.

### 2.3 Manager mode's external members (`backend/storage/manager.go`, `backend/api/handlers_manager.go`)

- **The roster is per manager**: one row per `(manager_user_id, member_email)` (`016_manager.up.sql:9-23`), lowercased and trimmed by constraint (`023_...up.sql:196-198`). `member_user_id` is NULL for a non-Paceday person (`016:13`).
- **A roster row reaches a team only through an assignment** keyed by `(manager_user_id, team_id, member_email)` (`022_...up.sql:1-10`). The team view joins the two (`storage/manager.go:158-183`). Another member of the same team never sees the manager's assignments.
- **Add** (`handlers_manager.go:293-360`): owner of the team when `team_id` is given (`:295`, `managerTeamScope` `:642-658`).
  - It resolves the address to the one domain-confirmed holder, if any (`:333-338`, PAC-71 granting site), and stores the account id. A lookup error stores NULL (SF-010).
  - The response is the stored row, **including `member_user_id`** (`:351-359`; struct `storage/manager.go:60-72`).
- **The team's manager view merges** formal members (as `source: "formal"`) with the manager's assignments (`handlers_manager.go:660-706`). `is_paceday_user` is `member_user_id != nil` (`:277`, `:620`).
- **External analytics** (`engine/manager.go:433-517`):
  - A Paceday user's minutes are shown only to an owner of a team the user is on (PAC-60, `:434-444`; `storage/teams.go:336-351`).
  - An external's week is read as free/busy **through the manager's credential** (`:484-502`): meeting minutes = busy minutes, free = 2400 − busy, no focus.
  - The result is **persisted** per `(manager, email, week)` (`:504-514`; table `016:34-46`).
- **Removal with `team_id`** (`:362-408`, PAC-122 SF-001). In one transaction it removes:
  - the formal memberships of every account carrying the address (PAC-71 revoking site, `:375-405`);
  - the assignment;
  - the team's pending invites for the address (`storage/manager.go:210-238`).

### 2.7 Privacy and existence

- **Team invite: no oracle today.** The answer is 201 for any valid address (`handlers_teams.go:207-234`). Accept reveals nothing about other accounts.
- **Manager add: an oracle today.** Whether the address belongs to a confirmed Paceday account is returned as `member_user_id` and shown as "On Paceday" (§2.3, §2.6). Any team owner can probe any address. This spec must not copy it. Whether to close it on the manager side is OQ-9.
- **Who sees whose minutes.** PAC-60 limits a Paceday user's per-person minutes to an owner of a team the user is on (`engine/manager.go:434-444`). Formal team analytics ignores that rule and shows every member's minutes to every member (`teams.yaml:1032-1033`). That is outside this spec (§5), but this spec must not widen it.
- **Erasure.** There is no erasure by address for anyone without an account. Booker erasure (PAC-40) covers only bookers on deleted links (`storage/booker_erasure.go:1-36`). Invite rows, manager roster rows, 1:1 occurrences and the analytics cache keep addresses until a team or account cascade. Removing a manager roster member only unassigns it when `team_id` is given (`storage/manager.go:201-208`). The global row, its occurrences and its cached weeks stay.

### 2.9 MCP and limits

- **The MCP server does not touch teams.** Its only availability tool reads the user's own free/busy (`mcp/tools.go:129-137`). No change.
- **Limits.**
  - No cap on team members, invites or roster size anywhere.
  - Find a time reads members at 4 concurrent reads with 15 s each (PAC-81).
  - Free/busy uses one batched provider call with a 5 s timeout (`freebusy_service.go:132-134`). Providers cap the number of addresses per free/busy call. The exact Google and Microsoft limits were not verified here (OQ-7).

## 3. Desired behaviour

**Adding.** On the team's member management, an owner can **add a person** by email, with an optional display name. Suggestions come from people in the owner's calendar, excluding anyone already on the team. The person is on the team at once:
- no email is sent;
- nothing is asked of the person;
- the owner is told the person was added, never that anything was "sent".

The answer is the same whether or not the address belongs to a Paceday account. Adding the same address again, in any letter case or with surrounding spaces, changes nothing and does not create a second entry. Adding the owner's own address, or the address of someone already a full member, is refused with a reason, and nothing changes.

**Two kinds of member.** Every team member is one of two kinds:
- a **Paceday member**, who joined by accepting an invitation;
- an **added person**, who did not.

The kind is decided **only** by whether the person accepted an invitation, **never** by whether an account exists for the address. Every member of the team sees the added person in the member list (name, email, "not on Paceday"), as they see Paceday members today [OQ-5].

**What an added person gets and gives.** Nothing:
- They are not told [OQ-3].
- They cannot see the team, even if they have a Paceday account.
- Their Paceday data is never read for this team: no analytics, no focus blocks, no habits, no settings.

Their availability comes **only** from free/busy read through the calendar credential of the owner who added them, which is exactly what that owner's calendar already shows [F, D-2]. If that credential cannot read their calendar, or their address is at a personal-mail domain, their availability is **unknown**, and the product says so. It never shows them as free.

**Find a time and availability.**
- Slots are searched across Paceday members (as today) and added people.
- An added person who is busy blocks a slot exactly as a Paceday member does. An added person whose availability is unknown does not block a slot but is counted as unknown.
- Each slot states how many members are free, busy and unknown. It no longer claims "N of N" (SF-054).
- No-meeting zones apply to the whole team as today.
- Scheduling a slot puts added people in the attendee list like everyone else.

**Analytics.**
- An added person's row is **availability-only**: meeting time from free/busy, no focus figures, no focus score. It is labelled so.
- Their figures are not stored beyond the existing short free/busy cache.
- Per-person rows for added people are shown only to the team's owners [OQ-6]. They are left out of team averages.
- Every row is attributed to the right member even when two members share a name (SF-053). A member with no measured week is "no data", not zero (SF-041).

**Inviting to Paceday.** Inviting stays a separate, explicit action, offered on an added person ("Invite to Paceday") and still available for an address not yet on the team:
- Inviting an address not on the team also adds the person [F, OQ-4].
- Inviting an address that already has a pending invitation for this team creates no second invitation and sends no second email (SF-012). The owner is told it is already pending.
- The display name typed with an invitation is kept (SF-051).
- When the person accepts, while signed in to an account holding that address confirmed (as today), their entry becomes a Paceday member. Same entry, one person, one membership, all at once. From then on their availability is read with their own credential, as for any Paceday member.

**Removing.**
- An owner can remove any member of either kind. Removing an added person also withdraws that team's pending invitations for the address, so an old link cannot bring them back.
- A Paceday member can still remove themselves. The last owner can still not be removed (PAC-25).
- The web says "removed" only when the server removed someone (SF-089).

**Limits.** A team holds at most a fixed number of members, both kinds together [OQ-7, default 50]. An add or invitation that would exceed it is refused with a reason. Concurrent adds never take a team past the limit.

**Demo mode.** A demo session can add, list, invite and remove people, and run Find a time with them, without any network call.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

Test layers:
- `[contract]`: a Go test against the HTTP handler and the contract's shapes.
- `[unit]`: a Go or Vitest test below the HTTP layer, or a web component test.
- `[e2e]`: Playwright against the compose stack (the fake Google serves free/busy).

**Adding**

AC-1. Given a team owned by O with no member at `pat@ext.example`, when O adds `pat@ext.example` with name "Pat", then:
- the team's member list read by O, and by any other member, contains exactly one entry for that address, of kind "added", named "Pat";
- no email is attempted (the test SMTP sink receives nothing);
- no invitation exists for that address on that team.  [contract]

AC-2. Given address A held by a domain-confirmed Paceday account U and address B held by nobody, when O adds A and then B, then the two answers are identical except for the address and name, and so are the two entries in the member list: same kind, no account identifier, nothing that differs between them. Today's Manager-mode add fails the same test (§2.7).  [contract]

AC-3. Given the add dialog in the web, when the owner types two characters of a contact's address, then calendar-contact suggestions appear, excluding addresses already on the team. Choosing one fills the email and the name. Submitting shows "added", never "sent" or "invited", and the person appears in the member list without a reload.  [unit] (web component), [e2e]

## 5. Explicitly out of scope

- **Granting an added person access.** They never see the team. A "viewer" or read-only member is a different feature.
- **Notifying an added person**, by email or in the app (OQ-3 default). If the owner wants it, it is a follow-up. An in-app notice to an existing account would itself reveal that the address has an account to whoever can observe the notice.
- **An operator erasure-by-address tool** covering team entries, invitations, manager rosters, 1:1 occurrences and the analytics cache (§2.7). This spec only guarantees that removal leaves no team-scoped record of the address (AC-14). The cross-cutting tool is OQ-10's follow-up.
- **Fixing formal team analytics' missing consent and owner check** for Paceday members (§2.7). This spec must not widen it, and does not narrow it either.
- **Treating unreadable Paceday members as unknown.** Their read uses their own credential and is pinned as free (§2.4). Changing that is OQ-2.
- **Manager mode's existence oracle** (OQ-9) and SF-010.
- **No-meeting zone operations**, their `x-uncertain` markers, API-059 and API-087.
- **The 09:00–18:00 UTC search window.**
- **"Frequent collaborators" suggestions before typing** (excluded by PAC-128 too).
- **PAC-128's create-team initial invitees.** That stays PAC-128's.

## 7. Rollback

- **Migration.** One additive migration is expected (the plan decides its shape). It is applied under PAC-116's stop-traffic rule (`docs/runbook.md:98-130`).
- **An image-only rollback does not work.** The previous image cannot start on the newer database (`runbook.md:250-265` pattern).
- **Going back.** Either fix forward, or restore the pre-deploy backup and start the previous image.
- **The down migration loses data.** Every added person is deleted. Their names exist nowhere else, and those people drop out of Find a time. Invitations and Paceday memberships are untouched. A person added and then upgraded by acceptance is an ordinary membership, and survives.
- **Release note for a rollback:** owners must re-add the people they had added. Pending invitations created by AC-18 survive and can still be accepted.
- **Web rollback alone**, with the backend kept, hides added people from the old UI. The old web lists only account members (§2.6), so nothing breaks, but the owner cannot see or remove them. The old web's remove throws "team member has no user id" for any entry without an account. **The web must not be rolled back alone.**
